Given     The Needs you row offers "Decide" followed by an [[adr]] id, while [[ADR-0007]] says decisions are never asked in chat. A row that seems to ask for a decision in chat leads to [[cryptic-questions]].
Decide    What can the Needs you row of a [[response|reply]] ask of you?

Where A:  When writing the Needs you row, the [[agent]] asks you to Approve an action or Do a step, or points to a [[question]] already open in the dialog, and never asks for a decision in the row itself.
          → decisions stay in the dialog, and the row still reminds you of them · one more [[rule]] to remember
Where B:  When writing the Needs you row, the [[agent]] asks you to Approve an action or Do a step, and leaves [[question|questions]] in the dialog out of the row.
          → the row holds only actions · a [[question]] you closed is not mentioned again in the [[response|reply]]
Combines  exclusive
Recommend A: you see everything waiting on you in one row, and decisions still happen only in the dialog.

Decision  A
