Given     [[ADR-0010]] says a decided amendment takes the amended [[adr]]'s place, but in practice each amendment is folded into the original as a Consequences line, and both versions go to the Archive. A record kept one way and described another is [[hard-to-judge]].
Decide    What happens to an [[adr]] you read once an amendment to it is decided?

Where A:  When an amendment is decided, the [[agent]] moves the amended [[adr]] to the Archive and saves the amendment under the amended [[adr]]'s number, as a whole new [[adr]].
          → the new [[adr]] reads as one decision · the earlier options and reasons live only in the Archive
Where B:  When an amendment is decided, the [[agent]] archives both, then rewrites the amended [[adr]] under its own number with the decided [[rule]] as a Consequences line, citing the archived amendment.
          → one [[adr]] gathers every decision on its topic · a long-lived [[adr]] grows a long Consequences list
Combines  exclusive
Recommend B: it is what the Decisions folder already does, and it keeps each topic in one place.

Decision  B
