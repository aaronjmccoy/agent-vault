Given     The cheat sheet and six [[term]] notes use a decided_by field, but [[ADR-0003]]'s list of frontmatter fields and the [[term]] template leave it out. A field some notes have and others don't makes a [[term]]'s origin [[hard-to-judge]].
Decide    Does each [[term]] note you read say which decision introduced it?

Where A:  When writing a [[term]] note, the author gives it notation, type, aliases, version, status and decided_by, which names the [[adr]] that introduced the word, or is left empty when none did.
          → every word traces to its decision · one more field to keep current
Where B:  When writing a [[term]] note, the author gives it notation, type, aliases, version and status, and the [[adr|ADRs]] that use the word are found through their [[link|links]].
          → fewer fields · where a word came from must be looked up
Combines  exclusive
Recommend A: the field is already in use, and it answers "why does this word exist" in one place.

Decision  A
