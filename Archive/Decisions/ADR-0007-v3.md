Given     Only the cheat sheet says `/decisions` brings back [[question|questions]] left unanswered; [[ADR-0007]] says only that they are asked "again later", and the base prompt names no trigger. A [[question]] nobody brings back stays open forever, which is [[rules-not-landing]].
Decide    How does a [[question]] you left unanswered come back to you?

Where A:  When a [[question]] stays proposed, the [[agent]] asks it again after you run `/decisions` and write to it again.
          → you choose when to deal with waiting [[question|questions]] · they wait until you remember to ask
Where B:  When a [[question]] stays proposed, the [[agent]] asks it again at the start of its next turn, before other work.
          → nothing waits unseen · a [[question]] you put off returns every turn until answered
Combines  A and B combine: the [[agent]] asks again at the start of its next turn, and `/decisions` also brings them back on demand.
Recommend A: you decide when to return to a choice you set aside.

Decision  B
