Given     [[ADR-0001]] and the base prompt allow up to four options, matching the dialog, but the cheat sheet sets no limit. Too many options make a [[question]] [[hard-to-judge]].
Decide    How many options can a [[question]] put to you?

Where A:  When writing a [[question]], the [[agent]] gives it two to four options, so the whole [[question]] fits one dialog.
          → every [[question]] is answered in one place · a choice with more paths is narrowed first or split into two [[question|questions]]
Where B:  When writing a [[question]], the [[agent]] gives it as many options as the choice has, and spreads them over several dialogs when there are more than four.
          → no option is left out · one choice spans several dialogs, which is harder to compare
Combines  exclusive
Recommend A: one dialog per [[question]] keeps the options side by side.

Decision  A
