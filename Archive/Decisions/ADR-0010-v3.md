Given     Archived versions are named Draft-0001, Draft-0002 and so on, and retired [[term|terms]] go to a folder for them in the Archive, but no record says so. An archive whose naming is unstated is [[hard-to-judge]] when you look something up.
Decide    How are the archived versions you can look up named?

Where A:  When the [[agent]] archives an [[adr]], it renames it to the next number in one Draft series, such as ADR-0010-v1, notes which [[adr]] replaced it, and moves retired [[term|terms]] to the Archive under their own names.
          → one simple running series · a draft's number says nothing about which [[adr]] it belonged to
Where B:  When the [[agent]] archives an [[adr]], it keeps the [[adr]]'s id with a version suffix, such as ADR-0004-v3, notes which [[adr]] replaced it, and moves retired [[term|terms]] to the Archive under their own names.
          → you see at a glance which [[adr]] a version belongs to · the existing drafts must be renamed
Combines  exclusive
Recommend B: the history of each [[adr]] sorts together, which is what you look for in an archive.

Decision  B
