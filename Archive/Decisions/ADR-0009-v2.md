Given     [[ADR-0009]] keeps both [[agent|agents]] to the [[contract]] with no prose, but the base prompt and cheat sheet only bind the receiving [[agent]]. Prose added by the handing [[agent]] is as [[hard-to-judge]] as prose sent back.
Decide    Can the [[agent]] that hands over a [[job]] add words of its own to the [[contract]]?

Where A:  When handing a [[job]] to another [[agent]], the [[agent]] sends the [[contract]] alone, and every instruction it needs to give is a [[requirement]] in that [[contract]].
          → everything asked can be checked · context that is not a [[requirement]] has nowhere to go
Where B:  When handing a [[job]] to another [[agent]], the [[agent]] sends the [[contract]] with up to two sentences of context, such as where to start, that state no [[requirement]].
          → the receiver gets its bearings quickly · the context itself is never checked
Combines  exclusive
Recommend B: a pointer to where to start saves the receiver time without asking for anything unchecked.

Decision  A
