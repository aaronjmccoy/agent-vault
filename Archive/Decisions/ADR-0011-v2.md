Given     When a recomposed [[response|reply]] still breaks the reply [[rule]], it is shown with a ⚠ notice and the [[agent]] is told at its next turn, but no [[rule]] says what the [[agent]] does then, which is [[rules-not-landing]].
Decide    What do you see from the [[agent]] after a [[response|reply]] of its own was shown with a ⚠ notice?

Where A:  When a reply check notice reaches the [[agent]], the [[agent]] begins its next [[response|reply]] by recomposing the flagged [[response|reply]] in the shape the notice asks for, then answers the new request.
          → the broken [[response|reply]] is replaced by a correct one you can read · the next [[response|reply]] repeats earlier content and runs longer
Where B:  When a reply check notice reaches the [[agent]], the [[agent]] applies what the notice says from its next [[response|reply]] onward and leaves the flagged [[response|reply]] as it was shown.
          → replies stay short and move on · the flagged [[response|reply]] stays on screen with its ⚠ notice
Combines  exclusive
Recommend A: you asked that a notice be attended to by regenerating the [[response|reply]], and A makes that the [[rule]].

Decision  A
