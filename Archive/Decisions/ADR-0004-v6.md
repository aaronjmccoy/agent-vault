Given     The base prompt and template say Done and Open may repeat, the reply check also allows Needs you and Check only once, and [[ADR-0004]] and the cheat sheet say neither. Unstated limits make a [[response|reply]] [[hard-to-judge]] against the [[rule]].
Decide    Which rows can appear more than once in a [[response|reply]] you read?

Where A:  When writing a [[response|reply]], the [[agent]] repeats Done and Open as needed, one item per row, and uses Needs you and Check at most once each.
          → one thing waits on you and one way to check, so neither gets lost · several approvals share one row
Where B:  When writing a [[response|reply]], the [[agent]] repeats any row as needed, one item per row.
          → each approval and each check gets its own row · several Needs you rows can bury the one that matters
Combines  exclusive
Recommend A: it keeps what waits on you to a single row.

Decision  A
