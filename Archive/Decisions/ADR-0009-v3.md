Given     The [[message]] note defines a YAML handoff record (who, what [[job]], status, [[link|links]] and needs), while [[ADR-0009]] says [[agent|agents]] hand each other only a [[contract]]. Two handoff formats make a handoff [[hard-to-judge]].
Decide    What role does a [[message]] play in a handoff you can inspect?

Where A:  When handing a [[job]] to another [[agent]], the [[agent]] wraps the [[contract]] in a [[message]] that names who sends it, the [[job]], its status and a [[link]] to the [[contract]], and asks for nothing the [[contract]] does not.
          → every handoff has a traceable envelope · one more record per handoff
Where B:  When handing a [[job]] to another [[agent]], the [[agent]] sends the [[contract]] with no envelope, and the [[message]] [[term]] is retired to the Archive.
          → one format only · who sent what, and when, is not recorded
Combines  exclusive
Recommend A: the envelope records the handoff without adding a second set of instructions.

Decision  B
