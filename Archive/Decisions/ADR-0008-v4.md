Given     The [[verdict]] note calls a [[verdict]] a YAML record, but [[ADR-0008]], the base prompt and the cheat sheet never say what form it takes or where it is kept. A [[verdict]] you cannot find or read is [[hard-to-judge]].
Decide    What form does a [[verdict]] you read take?

Where A:  When the [[verifier]] writes a [[verdict]], it saves it as a YAML record next to the [[spec]], and its [[response|reply]] gives a [[link]] to it in the Check row.
          → a lasting record that tools can read · you open a file to see the details
Where B:  When the [[verifier]] writes a [[verdict]], it puts it in its [[response|reply]] as a table of [[requirement|requirements]], each with its [[result]] and [[evidence]].
          → the outcome is right there in the chat · nothing is kept once the conversation ends
Combines  A and B combine: the [[verifier]] saves the YAML record and also shows the table in its [[response|reply]].
Recommend A: a [[verdict]] is worth keeping, and the Check row puts it one click away.

Decision  A
