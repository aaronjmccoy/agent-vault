Given     Most [[person|people]] ask an [[agent]] to "check your work" and expect it to know what that means. A check whose [[scenario|scenarios]] were never agreed is [[hard-to-judge]]: a pass says nothing about what was left out.
Decide    What must happen when a [[person]] asks for a check?

Where A:  When a [[person]] asks for a check, the [[agent]] shall first list the [[requirement|requirements]] and [[scenario|scenarios]] it will test as a compact table and confirm them with the [[person]], and the [[verifier]] shall then check the [[job]]'s change against them and write a [[verdict]], with [[evidence]] for each [[result]] and at most five [[finding|findings]].
          → you see exactly what "checked" will cover before it runs, and a pass means what you agreed · one confirmation step per check
Where B:  When a [[person]] asks for a check, the [[verifier]] shall check the [[job]]'s change against its [[spec]] and write a [[verdict]], with [[evidence]] for each [[result]] and at most five [[finding|findings]].
          → no confirmation step · what was tested is only visible afterwards
Combines  exclusive
Recommend A: it is what you asked for: a check starts by agreeing what will be tested.

Decision  A
