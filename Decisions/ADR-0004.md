Given     The earlier version of this rule ([[Draft-0017]]) said a [[response|reply]] uses four labelled lines, but the cheat sheet, the base prompt's [[response|reply]] section and the [[response|reply]] template all show a two-column table of labelled rows. When a [[rule]] and its descriptions disagree, a [[response|reply]] is [[hard-to-judge]] against either.
Decide    Should the [[response|reply]] [[rule]] describe the table that replies now use?

Where A:  When writing a [[response|reply]], the [[agent]] uses a two-column table of up to four labelled rows (Done, Open, Needs you, Check), each a [[link]] or one short clause, in at most 8 rows.
          → the [[rule]], the cheat sheet and the base prompt all say "rows" · the [[response|reply]] note is reworded to match
Where B:  When writing a [[response|reply]], the [[agent]] uses four labelled lines (Done, Open, Needs you, Check), each a [[link]] or one short clause, in at most 8 lines, laid out as a two-column table.
          → the [[rule]] keeps its wording and names the table as layout · "lines" and "rows" both stay in use for one thing
Combines  exclusive
Recommend A: one word for one thing, matching what you see in every [[response|reply]].

Decision  A
