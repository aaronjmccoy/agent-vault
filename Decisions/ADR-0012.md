---
id: ADR-0012
status: proposed
scope: agents/handoff
---
Given     A [[message]] is defined as the record one [[agent]] writes to hand a [[job]] to another, but no [[rule]] calls for it. Handoffs to subagents are written as free prose, so what was asked and what came back is [[hard-to-judge]].
Decide    Must an [[agent]] that hands a [[job]] to another [[agent]] write a [[message]]?

Where A:  When an [[agent]] hands a [[job]] to another [[agent]], the [[agent]] shall write a [[message]] naming the [[job]], its status, its [[link|links]] and its needs, and the receiving [[agent]] shall answer with one.
          → every handoff can be traced, both ways · more overhead for small lookups
Where B:  When an [[agent]] hands a [[job]] with a [[spec]] to another [[agent]], the [[agent]] shall write a [[message]] naming the [[job]], its status, its [[link|links]] and its needs.
          → handoffs of real work are traced · quick lookups stay as free prose
Where C:  Where an [[agent]] hands work to another [[agent]], the [[agent]] shall write the handoff in its own words.
          → nothing changes, and [[message]] stays as vocabulary · handoffs stay hard to trace
Combines  exclusive
Recommend B: it records the handoffs you would want to audit, and skips the ones nobody reads.
