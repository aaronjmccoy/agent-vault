Given     You set this on 2026-10-21: "agents should only communicate via their work contracts using Gherkin. Eliminate prose and idle diction." Handoffs written as free prose are [[hard-to-judge]], both what was asked and what came back.
Decide    How must an [[agent]] hand a [[job]] to another [[agent]]?

Where A:  When handing a [[job]] to another [[agent]], the [[agent]] hands it as a [[contract]], and both [[agent|agents]] communicate only through that [[contract]] and a [[result]] for each of its [[scenario|scenarios]], with no prose.
          → every handoff states what must be done and how it is checked, and what comes back can be checked against it · each handoff needs a [[spec]], even a small one
Where B:  When handing a [[job]] to another [[agent]], the [[agent]] writes the handoff in its own words.
          → quick to write · what was asked and what came back stay [[hard-to-judge]]
Combines  exclusive
Recommend A: it is the rule you already set.

Decision  A
