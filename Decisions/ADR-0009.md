Given     Handoffs written as free prose are [[hard-to-judge]]: neither what was asked nor what came back can be checked. A [[contract]] in [[gherkin|Gherkin]] states both, so each can be checked.
Decide    In what form does an [[agent]] hand a [[job]] to another [[agent]], which decides what you can check of the handoff?

Where A:  When handing a [[job]] to another [[agent]], the [[agent]] hands it as a [[contract]], and the receiving [[agent]] answers with a [[result]] for each of its [[scenario|scenarios]], with no prose.
          → what comes back can be checked against what was asked · each handoff needs a [[spec]], even a small one
Where B:  When handing a [[job]] to another [[agent]], the [[agent]] hands it as a [[contract]], and the receiving [[agent]] answers with a [[response|reply]] table, with no [[result]] for each [[scenario]].
          → the answer reads like every other [[response|reply]] · what came back cannot be checked [[scenario]] by [[scenario]]
Combines  exclusive
Recommend A: a [[contract]] fixes what is asked and how it is checked before the work starts.

Decision  A
