Given     Unclear intent hides inside undefined words. Linking every noun to a [[term]] note makes a missing definition visible, and a script can check for one.
Decide    What may appear in a [[spec]] line, and how is each [[term]] addressed?

Where A:  When writing a [[spec]] line, the author uses only [[ears|EARS]] and [[gherkin|Gherkin]] keywords, the fixed operators (is, equals, above, below, within, contains, not) and [[selector]]s, and links every other noun as a short [[term]] link.
          → readable and checkable · undefined words show up as missing notes
Where B:  When writing a [[spec]] line, the author links every word, verbs included, by its full address.
          → completely uniform · hard to read and costly for [[agent|agents]]
Combines  exclusive
Recommend A: strict where it matters, readable everywhere else.

Decision  A
Consequences  Each [[term]] has one note. Its folder is its namespace, and a link is qualified only when two names collide. Frontmatter follows W3C SKOS: notation (a stable id), aliases, broader, a one-sentence definition, a type and a version. The type lives inside the note, never in the address. Changing what a [[term]] means bumps its version, and [[agent|agents]] use only the newest one.
