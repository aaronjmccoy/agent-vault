Given     [[agent|Agent]] [[question|questions]] turn cryptic when they bring in jargon with no introduction, give options without an actor or a trigger, and read the [[person]]'s words as fixed picks.
Decide    What shape must an [[agent]]'s [[question]] to a [[person]] take?

Where A:  When asking a [[person]] to decide, the [[agent]] writes the [[question]] as an [[adr]] with status proposed, in the lines Given, Decide, Where (one per option), Combines and Recommend.
          → the answer becomes the record · each option is the exact [[rule]] that would be adopted
Where B:  When asking a [[person]] to decide, the [[agent]] asks in prose and records the outcome afterwards.
          → quicker to write · the [[question]] and the record drift apart, and options stay vague
Combines  exclusive
Recommend A: the answer becomes the record, and each option is the exact [[rule]] that goes into force.

Decision  A
Consequences  Each Where option is written as an [[ears|EARS]] [[rule]]. The Decide line says what changes for the [[person]], not only the mechanism. Every new [[term]] is linked to a [[term]] note before a [[question]] uses it. An [[agent]] does not ask what it can look up, and does not ask when a sensible default exists. Answers come as an option, a [[rule]] built in the form, or free text that an [[agent]] turns into a [[rule]].
