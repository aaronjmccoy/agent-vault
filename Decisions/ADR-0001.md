Given     A [[question]] asked in free prose turns [[cryptic-questions|cryptic]]: jargon without introduction, and options without an actor or a trigger.
Decide    What shape does each [[question]] an [[agent]] puts to you take?

Where A:  When asking a [[person]] to decide, the [[agent]] writes the [[question]] as an [[adr]] with status proposed, in the lines Given, Decide, Where (one per option), Combines and Recommend.
          → the answer becomes the record · each option is the exact [[rule]] that would be adopted
Where B:  When asking a [[person]] to decide, the [[agent]] asks in prose and records the outcome afterwards.
          → quicker to write · the [[question]] and the record drift apart, and options stay vague
Combines  exclusive
Recommend A: the answer becomes the record, and each option is the exact [[rule]] that goes into force.

Decision  A
Consequences
- When writing an option, the [[agent]] writes it as one [[ears|EARS]] [[rule]] in the present tense.
- When writing the Decide line, the [[agent]] names what changes for the [[person]], not only the mechanism.
- When a [[question]] uses a new word, the [[agent]] first writes its [[term]] note and links it.
- If the answer can be looked up, or a sensible default exists, the [[agent]] does not ask.
