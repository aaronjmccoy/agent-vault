Given     A [[question]] asked in free prose turns [[cryptic-questions|cryptic]]: its options have no actor or trigger, and nothing records what was chosen.
Decide    What shape does each [[question]] an [[agent]] puts to you take?

Where A:  When asking a [[person]] to decide, the [[agent]] writes the [[question]] as a proposed [[adr]] from the Question template: a Given, one Decide question, two to four Where options that each state an [[ears|EARS]] [[rule]] with what happens and what it costs, Combines, and Recommend.
          → each option is the exact [[rule]] that goes into force, and the answer becomes the record · every [[question]] takes a file to write
Where B:  When asking a [[person]] to decide, the [[agent]] writes the [[question]] as a proposed [[adr]] from the Question template: a Given, one Decide question, two to four Where options that each state an [[ears|EARS]] [[rule]] with what happens and what it costs, and Combines, with no Recommend.
          → you weigh the options with no nudge from the [[agent]] · there is no pick to accept quickly, and the [[agent]]'s view is lost
Combines  exclusive
Recommend A: the [[agent]]'s pick and its reason allow a quick answer without hiding the other options.

Decision  A

Consequences
- When a choice comes up, the [[agent]] asks only if it cannot look the answer up and no sensible default exists, and writes a [[term]] note first for any new word the [[question]] uses ([[ADR-0001-v6]]).
- When writing a [[question]], the [[agent]] writes a Given of at most two sentences that says why the [[question]] came up and names the problem it serves ([[ADR-0001-v2]]).
- When writing a [[question]], the [[agent]] writes the Decide line as one sentence ending in "?" that names what changes for the [[person]], not only the mechanism ([[ADR-0001-v3]]).
- When writing a [[question]], the [[agent]] gives it two to four options, so the whole [[question]] fits one dialog ([[ADR-0001-v4]]).
- When writing an option, the [[agent]] writes one [[ears|EARS]] [[rule]] in the present tense that follows the [[rule|rules]] in force and differs from the other options only in the course of action it suggests.
- When writing a [[question]], the [[agent]] states in a Combines line which options can be picked together, and the dialog lets you pick several when they combine ([[ADR-0001-v5]]).
