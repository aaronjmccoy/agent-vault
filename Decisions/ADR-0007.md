Given     A [[question]] can be answered in a separate pane, which cannot be opened on mobile, or in the client's native [[question]] dialog. The dialog has a prompt, 2–4 options that each carry a label and a description, the recommended option first, and a free-text Other. Answering in place serves [[hard-to-judge]], because the choice sits next to the work it is about.
Decide    Where should you answer an [[agent]]'s [[question|questions]]?

Where A:  When asking a [[person]] to decide, the [[agent]] writes the proposed [[adr]] and shows it as the native [[question]] dialog, and the dialog writes the [[person]]'s pick back into that [[adr]].
          → you answer in place, and the separate pane is removed · at most 4 options, no hover definitions, and nowhere for an unanswered [[question]] to wait
Where B:  When asking a [[person]] to decide, the [[agent]] writes the proposed [[adr]] and ends its turn, and a separate pane shows it for the [[person]] to answer.
          → definitions on hover, and deferred [[question|questions]] wait in the pane · you must open the pane to answer, and it does not open on mobile
Where C:  When asking a [[person]] to decide, the [[agent]] writes the proposed [[adr]] and shows it as the native [[question]] dialog, and a separate pane keeps any [[adr]] left unanswered or deferred.
          → you usually answer in place, and nothing is lost if you close the dialog · the same [[question]] can appear in two places
Combines  exclusive
Recommend C: the dialog covers the usual case, and the pane only holds what you set aside.

Decision  A
