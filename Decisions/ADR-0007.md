Given     A [[question]] answered away from the work it is about is [[hard-to-judge]]. The client's native question dialog opens beside the chat on desktop and mobile, with up to four options, the recommended one first, and a free-text Other.
Decide    Where do you answer an [[agent]]'s [[question|questions]]?

Where A:  When asking a [[person]] to decide, the [[agent]] writes the proposed [[adr]] and shows it as the native [[question]] dialog, and the dialog writes the [[person]]'s pick back into that [[adr]].
          → you answer in place, on desktop or mobile · at most four options per [[question]], and a closed dialog leaves the [[adr]] proposed until it is asked again
Where B:  When asking a [[person]] to decide, the [[agent]] writes the proposed [[adr]] and puts its id in the Needs you row of its [[response|reply]], and the [[person]] writes the pick into that [[adr]].
          → you answer when you like · you must open and edit the file, which is slow on mobile
Combines  exclusive
Recommend A: you answer where the work is, and the pick is written back for you.

Decision  A

Consequences
- When asking, the [[agent]] never asks in chat; it puts up to four waiting [[question|questions]] in one dialog, one per [[adr]], each with its header set to the [[adr]] id ([[Draft-0046]]).
- If the [[person]] types in Other instead of picking, the [[adr]] stays proposed, and the [[agent]] reads what they mean, talks it through or revises the [[adr]], and asks again.
- When a [[question]] stays proposed, the [[agent]] asks it again at the start of its next turn, before other work ([[Draft-0047]]).
