Given     Questions were answered in a separate pane drawn by the mod, which cannot be opened on mobile. The client already has a native [[question]] dialog with a prompt, 2–4 options that each carry a label and a description, the recommended option first, and a free-text Other. Answering in place serves [[hard-to-judge]], because the choice sits next to the work it is about.
Decide    Where should you answer an [[agent]]'s [[question|questions]]?

Where A:  When an [[agent]] asks a [[person]] to decide, the [[agent]] shall write the proposed [[adr]] and show it as the native [[question]] dialog, and the decision-cards mod shall write the answer back into that [[adr]].
          → you answer in place, and the separate pane is removed · at most 4 options, no hover definitions, and nowhere for an unanswered [[question]] to wait
Where B:  When an [[agent]] asks a [[person]] to decide, the [[agent]] shall write the proposed [[adr]] and end its turn, and a separate pane shall show it for the [[person]] to answer.
          → as now: definitions on hover, and deferred [[question|questions]] wait in the pane · you must open the pane to answer, and it does not open on mobile
Where C:  When an [[agent]] asks a [[person]] to decide, the [[agent]] shall write the proposed [[adr]] and show it as the native [[question]] dialog, and a separate pane shall keep any [[adr]] left unanswered or deferred.
          → you usually answer in place, and nothing is lost if you close the dialog · the same [[question]] can appear in two places
Combines  exclusive
Recommend C: the dialog covers the usual case, and the pane only holds what you set aside.

Decision  A
