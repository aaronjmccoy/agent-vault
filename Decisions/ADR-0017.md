Given     [[ADR-0009]] says [[agent|agents]] pass a [[job]] only as a [[contract]] and a [[result]] for each [[scenario]], but not what a receiving [[agent]] does when it is blocked or needs a decision. Without a [[rule]], it may guess, or put [[cryptic-questions]] to you from a session you never started.
Decide    Who asks you when an [[agent]] working on a handed-off [[contract]] needs a decision?

Where A:  If a receiving [[agent]] cannot meet a [[scenario]] without a decision, it returns a fail [[result]] for that [[scenario]], and the handing [[agent]] writes the [[question]] and asks the [[person]].
          → every [[question]] comes from the session you are talking to · a round trip between [[agent|agents]] before you are asked
Where B:  If a receiving [[agent]] cannot meet a [[scenario]] without a decision, it writes the [[question]] and asks the [[person]] in the dialog itself.
          → the [[agent]] that knows the problem asks it, at once · [[question|questions]] arrive from sessions you did not start
Combines  exclusive
Recommend A: every [[question]] comes from the session you are talking to, and the [[contract]] stays the only channel between [[agent|agents]].


Consequences
- If a receiving [[agent]] cannot meet a [[scenario]] without a decision, it returns a fail [[result]] for that [[scenario]], and the handing [[agent]] writes the [[question]] and asks the [[person]] ([[ADR-0017-v2]]).

Decision  A
